NORTHBRIDGE LOGISTICS - STAFF HANDBOOK, FINANCE CHAPTER (SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT; FICTIONAL COMPANY)

F.1 Purpose
This chapter explains how employees request, approve and settle expenses. It applies to all permanent
staff and contractors with a company card. Questions go to the finance helpdesk.

F.2 Definitions
"Expense report" means a claim for reimbursement of costs already paid by an employee. "Purchase request"
means an approval to buy something in the future. "Travel booking" means a trip booked through the travel
desk portal. These are three different processes with different approvers.

F.3 Purchase requests
Purchase requests up to 1000 EUR are approved by the budget owner. Requests above 1000 EUR need a second
approval from procurement. Requests above 25000 EUR also need a signed quote from the supplier.

F.4 Travel bookings
Travel bookings are approved by the line manager. A single travel booking above 5000 EUR is approved by the
travel desk manager instead of the line manager. Business-class flights always need the travel desk
manager's approval regardless of price.

F.5 Expense reports
F.5.1 Submit expense reports within 30 days of the expense. Attach every receipt above 25 EUR.
F.5.2 Approval limits for expense reports: up to 500 EUR - the employee's team lead; above 500 EUR and up to
5000 EUR - the head of the department; above 5000 EUR - the regional finance director. An approver may not
approve their own report; it then goes to the next level.
F.5.3 Reports approved by the 25th of a month are reimbursed with that month's salary; later approvals are
paid with the next salary.

F.6 Company cards
Company card limits are 3000 EUR per month by default. Cash withdrawals with a company card are not allowed.
Lost cards must be reported to the card provider and to the finance helpdesk within 24 hours.

F.7 Audit
Finance audits a random 5 percent of expense reports every quarter. Missing receipts found in an audit must be
provided within 10 working days; otherwise the amount is deducted from the next salary.
